Setup

  1. Open accessibility settings (button on the first tab).
  2. Find PhoneAgent and turn it on.
  3. If it is grey (Android 13 and up): Phone Settings, Apps, PhoneAgent, three dots, "Allow restricted settings". Then try again.
  4. Come back and run a command.

Commands: open, tap, type, scroll up/down, back, home, wait 3, read screen. Join steps with "then".

This is not an AI model. It follows your commands. It stops on password screens and money apps, and asks before send, pay, delete, install. The red STOP button stays on screen.
